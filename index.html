Frequently asked questions

Questions before you apply?

Here are the essentials. Product-specific terms are confirmed during the application process.

Who can apply for a FastCred loan?
FastCred offers loans to eligible SMEs and individuals in Zimbabwe. Every application is assessed against the applicable eligibility, affordability and documentation requirements.
What interest rate will I pay?
Rates and fees depend on the loan product and credit assessment. FastCred discloses the applicable pricing and repayment terms before you accept a loan. Contact us for current terms.
How long is the repayment period?
Repayment periods vary by loan type and assessment. Your specific term and repayment schedule are confirmed before acceptance.
What documents are required?
Requirements depend on whether you are applying as an individual or an SME. Typical applications require identification, proof of income or business cash flow, and supporting documents relevant to the assessment.
How quickly will my application be processed?
Turnaround depends on the completeness of your application and the required credit checks. Submitting complete and accurate documents helps avoid delays.
