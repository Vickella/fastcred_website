Frequently asked questions

Start with the essentials

Which FastCred service should I choose?
Use Loan Services when you need finance, Business Systems for POS or accounting tools, and Advisory or Financial Planning when you need structured guidance. Contact us if your need crosses more than one area.
Who can apply for finance?
Eligible individuals and MSMEs in Zimbabwe may apply. Every request is considered against the relevant eligibility, affordability, documentation and credit requirements.
What rate and repayment period will I receive?
Pricing and terms depend on the product and assessment. Approved applicants receive the applicable rate, fees and repayment schedule before acceptance.
Can FastCred help if I do not need a loan?
Yes. FastCred also supports clients with business consultancy, POS systems, bookkeeping and accounting tools, financial planning and general financial guidance.
How do I get started?
Apply online for a loan or use the contact form for another service. A FastCred representative will respond with the relevant next steps.
