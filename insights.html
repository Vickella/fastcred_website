Practical financial insights

Short, useful guidance for business owners and individuals preparing to make better-informed financial decisions.

Business finance

Prepare before you apply for working capital

A clear purpose, recent records and a realistic repayment source make a business-finance request easier to assess.

  • Define exactly what the money will fund.
  • Gather sales, expense and cash-flow information.
  • Match the repayment plan to the business cycle.
Explore business finance
Cash flow

Profit and cash are not the same thing

A business can record a sale and still wait weeks for payment. Track when money actually enters and leaves the business.

  • List expected receipts by date.
  • Schedule supplier and operating payments.
  • Identify gaps before they become urgent.
Explore bookkeeping support
Personal finance

Test affordability before accepting credit

Look beyond the amount you receive. Understand the instalment, total repayable, fees and impact on the rest of your monthly budget.

  • Use income you can reasonably rely on.
  • Include existing financial commitments.
  • Leave room for unexpected expenses.

Business systems

Use transaction data to improve decisions

A consistent POS and bookkeeping routine can reveal popular products, busy periods, unpaid amounts and cost pressure.

  • Record sales in one consistent system.
  • Reconcile cash and digital payments.
  • Review simple trends every week.
