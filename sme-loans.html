Business finance for working capital and growth

Purpose-led funding options for eligible micro, small and medium enterprises in Zimbabwe.

Business loan options

Match the facility to the business need

FastCred reviews the commercial purpose, supporting documents, cash flow and repayment ability behind every request.

Confirmed orders

Order Financing

Designed for eligible businesses that have a customer order but need short-term funding for stock, inputs or services required to deliver it.

  • Order or contract evidence
  • Supplier and fulfilment information
  • Repayment aligned to the transaction
Completed work

Invoice Discounting

Helps eligible suppliers access part of the value of a verified invoice before the customer settles it, supporting cash flow between delivery and payment.

  • Verified delivery and invoice
  • Customer payment terms reviewed
  • Discount pricing disclosed before acceptance
Productive capacity

Asset-Based Finance

Supports eligible asset purchases or working-capital requests backed by suitable business assets, subject to valuation and assessment.

  • Asset purpose or ownership evidence
  • Business cash-flow review
  • Security and valuation requirements may apply
Seasonal operations

Agribusiness Loans

Short-term finance for eligible agricultural inputs and working capital across a defined production or trading cycle.

  • Production plan and seasonal timing
  • Input or working-capital budget
  • Expected sales and repayment source
