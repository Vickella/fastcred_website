Privacy Notice

How FastCred handles information submitted through this website.

Website privacy

Your information

This notice applies to personal information submitted through FastCred's website forms.

Information we collect

When you submit a loan, service or contact enquiry, FastCred may collect the contact, application and supporting information you choose to provide. If you subscribe to updates, we collect your email address.

How we use it

Information is used to respond to enquiries, assess loan applications where applicable, deliver requested communications, request supporting documents, communicate outcomes and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

Do not send sensitive credentials

Do not submit passwords, PINs or payment-card security details through the website forms.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact info@fastcred.co.zw or visit 4th Floor Stanley House, 1st Street & Jason Moyo, Harare, Zimbabwe.

Important: This website notice should be read alongside FastCred's internal data-protection practices and reviewed periodically against applicable Zimbabwean legal requirements.
