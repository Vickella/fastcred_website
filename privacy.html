How FastCred handles information submitted through this website.

Website privacy

Your information

This notice applies to personal information submitted through FastCred's website forms.

Information we collect

When you submit a loan or contact enquiry, FastCred may collect the contact, application and supporting information you choose to provide.

How we use it

Information is used to respond to enquiries, assess loan applications where applicable, request supporting documents, communicate application outcomes and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

Do not send sensitive credentials

Do not submit passwords, PINs or payment-card security details through the website forms.
