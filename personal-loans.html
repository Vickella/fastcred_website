Individual loan options

Start with the purpose of the finance

Personal Loans

General-purpose personal credit for eligible applicants with a clear use, repayment source and affordability profile.

Salary-Based & SSB Loans

Finance for eligible salaried individuals, including qualifying civil-service applicants, assessed against verified income and affordability.

Emergency Loans

Support for eligible unforeseen expenses where the need is time-sensitive and the proposed repayment remains affordable.

School-Fee Loans

Finance structured around eligible education expenses for children or dependants, supported by the relevant fee information.

Application readiness

Prepare the information behind your request

FastCred may ask for more information depending on the product and assessment.

  • Valid identification and current contact details
  • Income and employment or repayment-source evidence
  • Existing financial commitments
  • Supporting invoice, quotation or school-fee statement where relevant

Plan the instalment first

Use the calculator as an early planning tool, then compare the result with the terms in any approved quotation.
