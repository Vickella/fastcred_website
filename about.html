About FastCred Financial Services

FastCred Financial Services is a registered microfinance institution serving SMEs and individuals in Zimbabwe.

Who we serve

Credit for businesses and individuals

FastCred provides SME and personal loan products through an application and credit assessment process designed to make requirements and loan terms clear.

Our role is to assess eligible credit needs responsibly and communicate the applicable pricing, fees and repayment schedule before an approved applicant accepts a facility.

What you can expect

Clear application requirements, responsible credit assessment and loan terms explained before you accept an approved facility.

FastCred serves eligible SMEs and individuals from Harare, Zimbabwe.

Our approach

Clear information at every stage

Clear communication

Application requirements and next steps are explained without unnecessary complexity.

Responsible assessment

Applications are considered against eligibility, affordability and relevant supporting information.

Documented terms

Approved applicants receive the applicable loan terms before accepting the facility.
