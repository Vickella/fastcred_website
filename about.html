A broader financial partner for Zimbabwe

FastCred combines responsible finance with practical business systems and guidance for individuals and MSMEs.

Who we are

Built to help clients make progress with confidence

FastCred Financial Services is a registered microfinance institution based in Harare. We serve individuals and growing enterprises with finance, financial know-how and tools that support better day-to-day decisions.

Our approach recognises that funding is only one part of financial progress. Strong records, useful systems, clear plans and informed decisions also matter, so our service range connects those needs in one place.

What clients can expect

Straightforward requirements, respectful support, responsible assessment and clear information before any approved credit facility is accepted.

Our mission

Help individuals and MSMEs turn sound ideas into sustainable progress.

We aim to make useful financial services easier to understand and access while supporting stronger financial habits and business operations.

Our vision

Be a trusted, practical financial-services partner in Zimbabwe.

We want clients to experience connected, technology-aware solutions that create clarity, capability and long-term value.

How we work

Values that shape the client experience

Simplicity

We make requirements and next steps easier to follow.

Integrity

We communicate honestly and treat client information responsibly.

Flexibility

We consider different needs without losing sight of sound assessment.

Efficiency

We value complete information, timely follow-up and practical action.

Innovation

We use better tools and ideas to improve the way clients operate.

Customer focus

We listen carefully and guide clients towards relevant solutions.

Who we support

Individuals and enterprises at different stages

Our services are designed for personal financial needs, early-stage enterprises, established MSMEs and operators who want better visibility over their finances.
